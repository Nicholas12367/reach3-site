## Section 4 — Recommended copy injections

Five surgical, low-risk additions to `/Users/nicholasconnelly/Applications/reach3-staging/index.html`. Each targets a high-value new keyword cluster from Section 3 without disrupting the existing brand voice.

### Injection 1 — Hero subtitle (lines 413–417)

**Why:** Hero currently says "Indoor digital screens in the restaurants, waiting rooms, shops, and service businesses your customers visit every single day across Lloydminster, Vermilion, and the Lakeland." It misses the broad commercial-intent searches — "out-of-home", "outdoor advertising", "local advertising".

**Suggested rewrite (replace the `<p>` on line 417):**
> Local advertising in Lloydminster done differently — an indoor out-of-home (OOH) screen network inside the restaurants, waiting rooms, shops, and service businesses your customers visit every single day across Lloydminster, Vermilion, Bonnyville, Cold Lake, St. Paul, and the wider Lakeland trading area.

Adds: *local advertising Lloydminster*, *out-of-home*, *OOH*, *trading area*.

### Injection 2 — Problem section H3 cards (the "Obvious Channels" comparison cards)

**Why:** The three card-titles already name social, print/flyers, billboards. Add a fourth card naming the other big competitor categories (radio, portable signs, newspaper) so the page picks up "alternative to" search variants in Section 3-B.

**Suggested addition — insert a fourth `card-title` H3:**
> Radio spots, portable signs, and newspaper ads — yesterday's playbook for yesterday's customer.

Adds: *radio advertising Lloydminster*, *portable signs Lloydminster*, *newspaper advertising Lloydminster*, *alternative to portable signs*.

### Injection 3 — "Why" / Math section — pricing comparison block

**Why:** Sign Advantage pitches "less than $0.01 per view." Big Sign sells in $300 / $550 / $900 packages. Pattison and VENDO use CPM. Our site has zero CPM/cost-per-view language even though screens are objectively cheaper per impression than any of those formats. Adding it captures comparison shoppers.

**Suggested addition — new paragraph inside the `#why` section, immediately after the "Math" headline:**
> Compare the math. Lloydminster billboards run $1,200–$2,500 a month. Radio spots cost $20–$60 each, gone in 15 seconds. Reach Screens delivers thousands of in-venue impressions a week at a fraction of a cent per view — a CPM that beats every traditional out-of-home, radio, and newspaper option in the Border City. Weekly and monthly packages available, with free ad design included.

Adds: *CPM Lloydminster*, *cost per view*, *billboard advertising Lloydminster*, *radio advertising Lloydminster*, *advertising packages*, *weekly / monthly advertising*, *free ad design*.

### Injection 4 — Areas section: expand the "Across Alberta & Saskatchewan" card

**Why:** Competitors rank for Wainwright, Provost, Macklin, Elk Point, Battlefords, College Park, West Lloydminster, downtown Lloydminster, Highway 16 / Yellowhead, Highway 17. Our areas section names the obvious towns but misses the long-tail neighbourhood and highway terms competitors are quietly winning.

**Suggested addition — append to the body of the "Across Alberta & Saskatchewan" card (or add a new "Hyper-Local Reach" card):**
> Plus deeper coverage across the surrounding trading area — Wainwright, Provost, Macklin, Elk Point, Two Hills, Vegreville, North Battleford, and the Battlefords — and inside every Lloydminster neighbourhood from downtown and College Park to West Lloydminster, along Highway 16 (the Yellowhead) and Highway 17.

Adds: *Wainwright advertising*, *Provost*, *Macklin*, *Elk Point*, *North Battleford*, *Battlefords*, *downtown Lloydminster*, *College Park*, *West Lloydminster*, *Highway 16*, *Yellowhead*, *Highway 17*.

### Injection 5 — New FAQ entry

**Why:** "Free ad design", "graphic design Lloydminster", "geofencing", and "place-based advertising" are all valuable adjacent searches with low competitor density.

**Suggested addition — add a new FAQ Q/A inside the `#faq` section:**
> **How is Reach Screens different from a billboard, radio spot, or geofencing campaign?**
> Billboards in Lloydminster cost $1,200–$2,500/month and get a three-second glance from a 100 km/h driver. Radio spots are gone in fifteen seconds. Reach Screens is place-based advertising — your message plays on indoor screens inside the cafés, gyms, salons, auto shops, and waiting rooms your customers already sit in for ten to forty minutes at a time. Free ad design and graphic design are included. No CPM games, no agency markup, no $900 monthly billboard minimum.
